THE CHAMPION SPIRIT SHINES ONCE AGAIN! 🏆🏀

Another thrilling victory, another remarkable milestone, and another championship proudly brought home by the St. Robert’s International College Panda Rackets Elite Lab!

With unwavering determination, exceptional teamwork, and an unrelenting desire for excellence, the St. Robert’s International College (SRIC) Panda Rackets Elite Lab emerged victorious against Hua Siong College of Iloilo (HSCI) in the MILO SBP/Passerelle Twin Tournament 2026 – Basketball Boys Championship, held on October 24, 2026, at the HSCI Ty Eng Liong Gym.

From the opening tip-off to the final buzzer, our Robertine cagers displayed remarkable athleticism, disciplined execution, and the heart of true champions. Every possession, every defensive stop, and every hard-earned basket reflected their dedication, resilience, and commitment to bringing honor to the SRIC community.

📊 Final Score: SRIC 75 – HSCI 69
📅 Date: October 24, 2026
📍 Venue: HSCI Ty Eng Liong Gym

Behind this championship triumph are countless hours of rigorous training, sacrifices, perseverance, and a shared commitment to greatness. This victory is a testament not only to the talent of our student-athletes but also to the dedication of their coaches, the unwavering support of their families, and the collective encouragement of the entire Robertine community.

To our outstanding athletes, dedicated coaching staff, and proud parents, CONGRATULATIONS! Your achievement has once again brought immense pride and honor to St. Robert’s International College. You have demonstrated that being a champion goes beyond winning games—it is about embodying discipline, courage, humility, sportsmanship, and excellence in every endeavor.

At SRIC, champions are not merely celebrated; they are developed, empowered, and inspired to achieve greatness.
