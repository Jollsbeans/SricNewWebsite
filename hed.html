PROGRAM LAUNCH

Cybersecurity Module Launch

St. Robert’s International College (SRIC), in partnership with Cusco Standardization International (CSI), the Engineering Council of Thailand (ECT), and Rajamangala University of Technology (RMUT), proudly launches its Cybersecurity Module - a pivotal milestone in advancing digital education.

FUTURE-READY LEARNING

Digital engineering modules

With strong support from the Region 6 offices of the Department of Education (DepEd) and TESDA, SRIC is introducing a bold and future-ready senior high school program. This initiative integrates four key Digital Engineering modules into the Grades 11 and 12 curriculum:

Cybersecurity
Supply Chain Management (to follow)
Smart City Development (to follow)
Digital Engineering (to follow)
INTERNATIONAL PATHWAYS

Certifications & opportunities

Delivered online by CSI and aligned with ISO 17024 standards, these programs are globally recognized across 175 countries. Assessments are administered by SRIC-CSI, with certifications awarded by International Qualification Certification Services (IQCS) Canada and Geman Cert, equipping students for globally competitive careers.

Through micro-credentialing, students gain advanced standing for the BS Digital Engineering degree at RMUT and access exclusive opportunities, including the Working Holiday Program in South Korea.

STUDENT SUPPORT

TESDA Vouchers & Congressional Funding

The Cybersecurity Module is a TESDA-approved program ready for nationwide implementation. Students may also benefit from TESDA scholarship/voucher programs and targeted congressional funding for SRIC learners:
