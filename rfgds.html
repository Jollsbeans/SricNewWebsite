International Business Program
Senior High School

The International Business Program (IBP) of St. Robert’s International College – Basic Education Department (SRIC BED-IBP) is a comprehensive K–12 academic offering that combines the national curriculum prescribed by the Department of Education (DepEd) with a globally-oriented, specialized curriculum. This unique academic design aims to nurture academic excellence, 21st-century skills, and international competencies among learners.

Senior High School Department: Features a Professional Development Program that bridges basic education with future career paths, preparing students for success in higher education and to thrive both in local and international work environments.

SRIC BED–International Business Program

St. Robert’s International College Basic Education Department- IBP-SHS (SMC) operates through the Open High School Program (OHSP) which is an alternative delivery mode offered to all learners inside and outside the Philippines. This is an academic program in conjunction with the K-12 Curriculum for learners who can adapt with varied mode of instruction like face to face, modular, independent learning or via online learning system. SRIC has secured and authorization for this operation from the DepEd Regional Office with document number RO-6 QAD-I- 176.

OUR SCHOOL COMMUNITY

The Robert F. Galindez Development School (RFGDS)

The Robert F. Galindez Development School (RFGDS) or Senior High School primarily aims to produce employable and college ready learners. The department offers the academic strands: General Academic Strand (GAS), Accountancy, Business and Management (ABM), Humanities and Social Sciences (HUMMS), Science, Technology, Engineering and Mathematics (STEM) and Information and Communications Technology (ICT) of the Technical- Vocational- Livelihood (TVL) Track. Industry exposure, practicum, and international immersion are given premium; as well as competence in Social Science, Natural Science and Business Research.

The Robertine Advantage: Graduate Job-Ready, University-Ready, and ISO Certified

Our cutting-edge Digital Engineering modules, backed by TESDA-funded ISO 17024 certification from German Cert - a global badge of personal competence. These certifications are not just diplomas - they’re real-world job tickets and university stepping stones, micro-credentialed with up to 1year of foundation subjects from Rajamangala University of Technology (RMUT).

Study details

Curriculum & Grading System

SRIC BED- IBP-SHS curriculum and grading system is in line with the standard DepEd Curriculum (DepEd Order No. 021, s. 2019, Policy Guidelines on the K-12 Basic Education Program). The Grade 11-12 Senior High School (RFGDS) follows the mandated DepEd Region VI Unified Senior High School Curriculum (DepEd Regional Memorandum Order No. 127, s. 2015).

Schedule of Classes/ Modality

SRIC BED-IBP-SHS has its own class schedules following the prescribed number of minutes per DepEd subjects and the monthly institutional enrichment activities .

Classes will be delivered online, offering students the flexibility to learn at their own pace, access materials anytime and from anywhere, and revisit content as needed to enhance understanding and retention. All online class sessions will be recorded and uploaded to the Learning Management System (LMS) for students to review at their convenience.

Learning Resources

The SRIC BED-IBP students are provided with access to library of learning resources—including PowerPoint presentations, worksheets, and interactive activities—through a designated Learning Management System (LMS). This platform serves as a centralized hub where students can conveniently access course materials, complete assignments, and engage in supplementary learning at their own pace, thereby supporting independent study and reinforcing concepts introduced in the recorded lessons.

Term Evaluations

Term evaluations are given twice each term—Midterm and Finals. These assessments are conducted online through the Learning Management System (LMS), allowing students to complete their exams in a structured digital environment that ensures accessibility, convenience, and timely submission of their responses.

BEYOND THE CLASSROOM

Co-Curricular Activities

Students will have a monthly in-person gathering, which may be academic in nature or designed for socialization, with the aim of fostering community building and strengthening the Robertine spirit among the learners. In addition, SRIC BED-IBP students are encouraged to participate virtually in institutional activities of SRIC-Iloilo City, such as the annual Robertine Week, SRB League Inter-Campus Competitions, and Year-End Rites.
