TESDA TRAINING MODULES

Explore the connected disciplines

Digital Engineering

Digital Engineering program provides students with a comprehensive foundation in the application of digital technologies across the engineering lifecycle. The course explores modern tools and practices such as systems modeling, data-driven design, automation, and simulation.

Explore Digital Engineering↗

Cyber Security

Cyber Security Qualification Program is a comprehensive and practical course designed to equip learners with the foundational and advanced skills necessary to protect digital systems, networks, and data from cyber threats. This course covers essential topics such as information security principles, network security, ethical hacking, risk management, cryptography, and incident response.

Explore Cyber Security ↗

Smart City

This course introduces students to the foundational concepts, technologies, and governance models that shape smart cities. It explores how digital innovation, data-driven decision-making, and sustainable design can transform urban environments into more efficient, livable, and resilient spaces.
