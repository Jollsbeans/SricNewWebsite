SAGE Philippines – St. Robert’s International College

SAGE World Cup 2016

Welcome to a proud moment in our journey of youth empowerment and innovation! In 2016, the global spotlight turned to the dynamic minds of young entrepreneurs at the SAGE World Cup and SAGE Philippines, represented by St. Robert’s International College, was front and center.

Our student leaders brought not just ideas, but impact. They showcased entrepreneurial projects that addressed real-world social and economic challenges, proving that age is no barrier to innovation and leadership.

SAGE World Cup 2016 wasn’t just about awards or recognition. It was about building a better future through socially responsible entrepreneurship. It was about connecting with youth leaders worldwide and showing that the Filipino spirit is a force for good.

At St. Robert’s International College, we believe in nurturing leaders who dream big, act boldly, and serve with heart. Our journey at the SAGE World Cup 2016 is a shining example of what our students can achieve on the world stage.
