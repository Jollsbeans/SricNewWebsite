Cyber Security Qualification Program

ROBERTINE CENTER FOR TECHNOPRENEURSHIP

Cyber Security
Qualification Program

Globally-Recognized. TESDA-Approved. Future-Ready.

This comprehensive, TESDA-recognized course equips learners with foundational to advanced cybersecurity skills. Through virtual labs, ethical hacking simulations, and hands-on projects, students develop technical expertise and critical thinking to protect digital infrastructures from cyber threats.

ABOUT COURSE

Build your cybersecurity skills

Total Duration: 405 Hours

1. BASIC COMPETENCIES – 45 Hours
  • a. Receive and respond to workplace communication
  • b. Work with others
  • c. Solve/address routine problems
  • d. Enhance self-management skills
  • e. Support Innovation
  • f. Access and maintain information
  • g. Follow occupational safety and health policies and procedures
  • h. Apply environmental work standards
  • i. Adopt entrepreneurial mindset in the workplace
2. COMMON COMPETENCIES – 90 Hours
  • a. Conduct a Cyber Risk Assessment
  • b. Develop and Implement Cybersecurity Policies and Standards
  • c. Apply Cybersecurity Governance Frameworks
  • d. Develop and Execute an Incident Response Plan
3. CORE COMPETENCIES – 90 Hours
  • a. Apply Network Security Fundamentals
  • b. Implement Endpoint Protection Measures
  • c. Design and Implement Secure System Architectures
  • d. Apply Cloud Security Best Practices
4. APPLIED COMPETENCIES – 90 Hours
  • a. Conduct Cyber Threat Intelligence (CTI) Activities
  • b. Perform Malware Analysis
  • c. Apply Digital Forensics Techniques
  • d. Manage Vulnerabilities in Information Systems
5. ADVANCED COMPETENCIES – 90 Hours
  • a. Develop and Conduct Cybersecurity Awareness Programs
  • b. Implement Social Engineering Defense Measures
  • c. Develop and Implement Disaster Recovery Planning
  • d. Develop and Maintain Business Continuity in Cybersecurity

Career pathways

Aligned with global frameworks, this course prepares students for roles in:

  • Cybersecurity Analysis
  • Ethical Hacking
  • IT Security Management

Delivered online with assessments by SRIC-CSI, certifications are issued by IQCS Canada and German Cert.

BEFORE YOU APPLY

Trainee Requirements

  • Senior HS student, college student, or professional
  • Of good moral character
  • Physically and mentally fit
  • Can operate basic computer systems
  • Proficient in oral/written communication

Explore the connected disciplines

Digital Engineering

Digital Engineering program provides students with a comprehensive foundation in the application of digital technologies across the engineering lifecycle. The course explores modern tools and practices such as systems modeling, data-driven design, automation, and simulation.

Explore Digital Engineering ↗

Supply Chain Management

Introduce students to the fundamental concepts, strategies, and practices involved in Supply Chain Management (SCM). It explores the flow of goods, information and finances from the point of origin to the final consumer.

Explore Supply Chain Management ↗

Smart City

This course introduces students to the foundational concepts, technologies, and governance models that shape smart cities. It explores how digital innovation, data-driven decision-making, and sustainable design can transform urban environments into more efficient, livable, and resilient spaces.
