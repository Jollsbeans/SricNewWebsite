SRIC Is ILOPRISAA 2024 3x3 BASKETBALL SECONDARY LEVEL CHAMPION

In the highly anticipated ILOPRISAA 2024 3x3 Basketball Tournament – Secondary Level, St. Robert's International College (SRIC) emerged as the overall champion, showcasing exceptional teamwork, skill, and determination throughout the competition. The final match was a thrilling showdown against the formidable team from the University of San Agustin (USA). SRIC maintained a strong lead for most of the game and sealed their victory with a final score of 18–12. This win not only highlighted the athletic excellence of the SRIC players but also demonstrated their commitment to discipline and sportsmanship. With this remarkable achievement, St. Robert's International College proudly adds another championship title to its growing legacy in interschool athletics.

SRIC Panda Rockets-Elite Lab Clinches First Runner-Up, Earns Coveted Spot at MOA Showdown

The NBTC 2024 Regional Championships in Bacolod recently concluded with an exhilarating showdown featuring 16 schools from the Visayas Region. The SRIC Panda Rockets-Elite Lab emerged as the first runner-up, securing a spot in the highly anticipated national finals at the SM Mall of Asia (MOA) Arena in March.

The Panda Rockets-Elite Lab's upcoming appearance at MOA Arena adds another layer of excitement to their already illustrious season. The young athletes from St. Robert's International College are ready to make their mark at the national finals, carrying the hopes and dreams of their supporters as they aim for glory on the grand stage of Philippine high school basketball.

Elementary SRIC Basketball Team Triumphs as Champions in ILOPRISAA 2024

It was a hard-fought and electrifying battle on the court between two determined teams — St. Robert's International College (SRIC) and Hua Siong College of Iloilo — during the ILOPRISAA 2024 Basketball Tournament, held at the John B. Lacson Foundation Maritime University (JBLFMU) Molo Gymnasium. From the opening tip-off, both teams displayed intense energy, remarkable athleticism, and fierce competitiveness, keeping spectators on the edge of their seats throughout the game.

Hua Siong fought valiantly, showing solid defense and offensive plays that tested SRIC’s resolve. However, SRIC remained composed and focused, demonstrating superior ball movement, strategic execution, and sharp shooting. In the final stretch, SRIC pulled ahead, maintaining their momentum and securing a well-earned victory with a final score of 62–52.

The match was a testament to the talent and sportsmanship of both squads, but it was St. Robert's International College that ultimately prevailed, adding another memorable win to their ILOPRISAA campaign. The game not only highlighted SRIC’s growing dominance in the tournament but also reflected the team’s unity, discipline, and commitment to excellence on and off the court.

Sports Development Program

Most schools have Academic Excellence as their top priority for all administrators and stakeholders. Sports Development Program is one of the interests that are considered supplementary to academic excellence. The main objective of the Sports Development Program is to give a tested framework for establishing a sports academy at St. Robert's International Academy. Sports College is a unique curriculum for athletes that offers a unique experience geared on developing talent while bolstering their academic skills.

The JCA Sports College of SRIC offers a venue to cater the multiple intelligences of the learner, improving their interpersonal and bodily-kinesthethic skill. Athletes will then be challenged to go beyond their sport and participate in a tailored program that combines practical training with interesting academic challenges. Real-world circumstances are used to apply lessons learned about socialization, teamwork, and planning. The Sports College does not neglect to instill ideals and foster camarederie among athletes from other sports, which is a quality of a great champion.

SRIC has made a name in the field of sports. Our players go on to have highly successful collegiate careers, as well as professional sports careers. The Sports College of SRIC uses the latest training techniques to maximize student-athlete potential. At the same time, thanks to the generosity of its community, students have access to sports facilities. SRIC employs a number of training and conditioning professionals to assure the health and well-being of its student-athletes as part of the school's objective to support students spiritually, intellectually, and physically.

Objectives:

  • To provide venue for students who are interested and wish to develop their knowledge and skills in sports.
  • To prove that pursuance of academic excellence can go “hand-in-hand” alongside sports excellence.
  • Boost the athletic competence of the learners in the field of sports.
  • To produce Champions in the field of Sports.
  • Create partnership with others school who wish to develop the skills and knowledge in sports of their learners.

Who are qualified to enroll under SRIA SPORTS ACADEMY?

  • Any Learner with or without experience can be part of the team.
  • All Sports Clinics are all open to Robertine learners from BCR- RFG, and outside learners of partner schools.
  • No grade requirements.
